About me

More than one skill. I sit where technology meets creativity: from fixing networks to shipping Android apps to designing the campaign.

Move Up Academy website

A full redesign of Move Up Academy's bilingual English school website: one-on-one lessons with Filipino teachers, Cebu study tours and AI after-class practice. I led the UI with the academy's in-house IT team and rebuilt every public page in a new navy-and-gold look.

  • Bilingual English / 中文 pages: home, summer and winter camps, long-term study, online program, curriculum, teachers, gallery, about and more.
  • Animated bee welcome intro, day/night mode, and a hero where teacher and student act out a real class conversation.
  • MOVE UP AI section with clickable tool cards and a sample lesson summary, showing lesson recaps, grammar quizzes and speaking feedback.
  • Courses by age and goal (kids, teens, adults, IELTS/TOEFL/KET/PET), a learning path with a free placement test, and a free-trial booking form.
  • An All Courses hub, plus a Log In popup that routes students and teachers to their own portals.
  • Now building the academy's class booking system: account login done, moving to MySQL.
